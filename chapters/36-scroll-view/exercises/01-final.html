Reading SVG

Scalable Vector Graphics describe pictures with shapes instead of pixels, so they stay sharp at any size.

A path is a list of commands: move, line, curve and arc. Most icons are a handful of paths.

Strokes can be dashed. When the dash is as long as the path, moving the offset draws the line.

Animations can follow time, user events or, as here, the scroll position of a box.

A scroll timeline turns the distance scrolled into animation progress, from 0% at the top to 100% at the bottom.

Browsers without scroll-driven animations simply skip it, so the article still reads fine.

Keep the ring small and out of the way: it is a hint, not the content.
